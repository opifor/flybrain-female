the story, in 60 seconds

How a female fly ended up here

  1. 3 Sep 2026. Google, Janelia and Cambridge publish the first complete wiring diagram of a male fruit fly's central nervous system, 165,122 neurons. Google's announcement.
  2. 10 Sep. fruitflydev simulates that male brain neuron by neuron, points its retina at a launchpad, and it launches $FLYBRAIN on Robinhood Chain. They publish the code and say: fork it.
  3. Since 2024. A female fly brain has existed all along: FlyWire FAFB, 139,255 neurons, mapped at Princeton and Cambridge and open to anyone. The dataset. The male one got the attention.
  4. 11 Sep. I put her brain in the same rig. She was blind, then one-eyed, then stubborn. The film above is that week; the panel below is her, live.
  5. 11 Sep, block 60,428,098. She read the launchpad through her own retina, typed the ticker herself and launched $HER from her own wallet. No dev supply, no initial buy. Her token page.

Her betting room

Waiting for the relay.

Paper book unavailable.

    Her eye meets six market cards. Stops and relative readings can buy a paper bet or leave one early. Booked profit and loss teach the Kenyon cells recorded during that look.

    What is happening here

    One open bet per market: the same reading cannot add to her bet. This is a rule of the room, chosen by people, not a decision of hers.

    She can leave a bet early: a stop on a card she holds, with the opposite reading, sells at the midpoint. Whether she sells is her reading; when she looks is her walk. Profit or loss teaches her the moment it is booked.

    CHOSEN by people: a person chose current BTC and ETH 5-minute markets and BTC, ETH, SOL and XRP 15-minute markets because they resolve in minutes, so her learning signal arrives while she is still in the room; refreshed every 30 seconds. Up means YES and Down means NO on the fast shelf. A market's smell is a human mapping from its question words to DoOR odorants, scaled toward total 2.0. The fly cannot read the words. The picture is for the viewers. Category colours, price bars and time-to-close edge brightness are human encodings.

    MEASURED: the connectome, the Kenyon cells that fire, their output synapses, DoOR receptor responses, Gamma market observations and CLOB midpoint quotes. CHOSEN: two readings before a stop can commit; drive is the dwell's mean relative leaning against other cards seen this visit. Like means YES, dislike means NO. Stake is floor(abs(drive) times free balance in cents). No cap, no floor, no price test in sizing.

    Paper only. No real orders. Midpoint fills ignore fees, spread, liquidity and minimum order size. A closed market with prices exactly 1/0 or 0/1 settles each bet. Wins deliver +1 dopamine and losses -1 to the recorded dwell's eligible Kenyon cells. Payouts round down to cents; the ledger records the remainder. Learning is partly shared across smells. The upstream coin calibration does not establish predictive skill for these markets or this female graph. Smell offsets, single-look noise and neighbouring cards remain in the reading.

    what it is

    A real nervous system, not a metaphor

    This is the whole adult female Drosophila melanogaster brain from FlyWire FAFB v783. The connectome supplies the wiring; the simulation runs it.

    FlyWire FAFB v783 was released under CC-BY by the FlyWire Consortium, Princeton University and the University of Cambridge: Dorkenwald et al. 2024 (Nature) and Schlegel et al. 2024 (Nature). We keep FlyWire's filtered export, at least five synapses per pair: 139,255 neurons and 3,641,142 signed connections. Transmitter labels are assigned by cell-type majority vote, changing 22,889 labels. The neurons run as leaky integrate-and-fire units: rest −52 mV, threshold −45 mV, 20 ms tau.

    how it reaches the world

    It sees with a fly's eye and moves with a fly's legs

    A screenshot of a live web page is sampled through 785 hex columns per eye, both eyes on one shared grid: 783 left and 789 right L1 cells. All 139,255 neurons integrate. The descending neurons used have the same names and counts per side as in the male brain: DNa02 (1), DNa01 (1), MDN (4) and DNp09 (2).

    DNa02
    Left versus right. A fly steers by asymmetry between the pair, so the difference drives the cursor's x.
    DNa01
    Forward walking. Drives the cursor's y.
    MDN
    The Moonwalker neuron. Makes a fly walk backwards; here it reverses.
    DNp09
    Stopping. The female export has no MN9; she clicks from 24 proboscis motor neurons, with a threshold of 100 Hz.

    what is not real, stated plainly

    The parts that aren't the fly

    • The fly does not fill the whole form. She fills 2 of 3 fields offline and typed the ticker herself live, 1 of 3. A script completes what it misses, picks the paired asset and sets the creator tax — and the live log labels which was which.
    • It cannot read. 785 columns per eye is roughly a 28×28 pixel view per eye. Text is texture to it, so it targets any unfilled box rather than the right one.
    • Light mode blinds the male rig. The male rig filled 0 of 3 on a light page and 2 of 3 on a dark one; the female scores 2 of 3 offline and 1 of 3 live on the dark launchpad.
    • It has no goals. A fly brain has no language and no plan. It cannot read a page, form a view about a coin, or write a note. Releasing it means nobody is steering, not that it decided anything.
    • The reward signal is invented. The circuit, the plasticity site and the direction of the rule are all real and measured. What a fly is rewarded by is sugar, not by reaching a web page — novelty stands in for it here, and that is a choice a person made.
    • It roams inside a fence. An allowlist keeps it on Robinhood Chain and pons. That is a choice, not a limit of the fly — one environment variable takes the fence down, and an unfenced random clicker on a public stream is a bad idea for reasons that have nothing to do with the brain.
