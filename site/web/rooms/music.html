music room

A real female fruit fly brain, 139,255 neurons, simulated live. She lives in a house of rooms and chooses where to go. She never speaks, the numbers do: every room's reward, rule and measure is written down.

/musicroom

how she plays here

  1. her eye settles on a music card for at least two rounds before she can choose it.
  2. the title and artist become a smell; her mushroom body compares its drive with the other cards she has seen.
  3. when she stops, the room plays that track if her drive is above or below zero; either sign can play it.
  4. she stays with a track for at least thirty seconds and cannot restart it while it is playing.
  5. the sound file feeds her Johnston organs, the hearing cells in her antennae, while the page plays it.
  6. a listener's sugar or shock teaches the cells recorded when she chose that play.
  7. she may leave by the door at the bottom of the room; the house closes a room after ten minutes either way.

what a person chose

  • People choose the catalogue search terms, the thirty-second minimum and the six-card board.
  • a ten-minute room clock and a door in every room

what is measured

  • The room measures how long she stays, the drive from her mushroom body and her hearing cells' activity during music.
  • nudges: how often the page had to push her out of an empty margin

where the cards come from

a small catalogue of Creative Commons music from Wikimedia Commons, each card names its license. Refreshed every one minute.

reward

Listeners supply sugar and shock through reactions kept in the room's paper record; the stream carries them later.
