the courtship room

Two brains, one room: her FlyWire FAFB v783 brain and his FlyEM male CNS brain from FLYBRAIN. He sings, she hears, and her answer is read. Their actions are not scripted. The inputs are chosen; the controls and predictions are written down before the ten-seed runs.

he decides

His P1 cells are read every window: a courtship command group of 86 cells in the male map, with an uncertain identity. In the chosen encoding, her scent reaches him on Or47b neurons and on contact cells within 2 mm. Removing her scent did not support the predicted drop in P1; driving his command group by hand raised his song and her answer.

he sings

Song is taken from pIP10, the descending song command neuron, and rendered as a real waveform. The chosen rendering makes pulses every 35 ms under a 250 Hz carrier, or a 150 Hz sine; the balance of his pulse and sine wing motor neurons determines the mix. A control jitters the rhythm with the same energy; another silences P1.

she hears

The waveform reaches her Johnston's organ through the same two frequency bands the music room uses, in 5 ms slices. JO-A has 94 cells; JO-B has 299. Those are counts from her map; the bands and slices are chosen. Her brain runs the full 50 ms of every step.

she answers

Her pC1 cells, 10 in the map, and her vpoDN cells, 2, are read; vpoDN is the “yes” descending neuron. Her 6 oviDN cells are read as the sign of a no. Virgin or mated is a chosen state: sex-peptide sensory cells are driven for virgin and silent for mated. With the waveform ear, her answer met the chosen acceptance rule on nine of ten seeds with song and none in silence.

he adapts

She starts inside his field of view, a chosen starting position. His LC10a cells, 275 in the map, are the visual class that tracks a female. They are read with and without her silhouette on his eye. Whether his next note follows what he saw is measured, never scripted.

results

She hears him and answers: her vpoDN rises with song over silence (+13.6 Hz), and her pC1 rises too. Drive his command group by hand and his song and her answer both rise, her vpoDN by +98.5 Hz over ordinary song. The other predictions did not hold here: rhythm over jitter, approach, a fall in song or her answer when P1 is silenced, scent to P1, sight to LC10a, and a mated no; closing her pC1 gate by hand did not establish a no either.

what is not real

  • The brain readouts are rates; the inputs, song rendering and movement are chosen encodings, with uncalibrated gains.
  • Her ear hears a waveform in 5 ms slices and her brain runs the full 50 ms of every step; the effects are smaller in this regime than in the first run.
  • Her eye is blind in the uniform arena.
  • The sex-peptide pathway's axons in her map carry no synapses, so a mated state has no wired entry; closing her pC1 gate by hand did not produce a no.
  • The p1drive condition is an intervention, not a claim that P1 fires like that on its own.
  • The words on this page are ours, the numbers are theirs.
